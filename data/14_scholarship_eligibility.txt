MERIT SCHOLARSHIP
The Demo University merit scholarship is available to eligible full-time undergraduate students. The standard renewal requirement is a cumulative GPA of at least 3.5 and completion of at least 24 credit hours during the academic year. Students must also remain in good academic and disciplinary standing. Renewal is reviewed at the end of each academic year. The Scholarship Office publishes application dates and required documents through the Student Portal.
