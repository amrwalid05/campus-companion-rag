CAREER SERVICES
Career Services supports students and recent graduates with CV reviews, interview practice, career fairs, and employer information sessions. Individual appointments can be booked through the Career Portal. Students should upload a draft CV at least 24 hours before a scheduled review. Career Services can provide guidance but does not guarantee employment or placement. Announcements about recruitment events are sent to university email accounts.
