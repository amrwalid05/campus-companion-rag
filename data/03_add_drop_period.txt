ADD AND DROP PERIOD
The add/drop period lasts for the first two weeks of the semester. During this period, students may add or drop a course through the Student Portal without an academic penalty, provided the change does not violate program requirements. After the deadline, a student must follow the formal withdrawal procedure. Tuition adjustments, if any, are handled by the Finance Office according to the published refund schedule. Students should save the portal confirmation.
