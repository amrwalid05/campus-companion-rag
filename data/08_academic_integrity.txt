ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
Academic misconduct includes plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, impersonation, falsification of data, and use of prohibited materials during an examination. Suspected cases are referred to the Faculty Academic Integrity Officer. Depending on severity and prior cases, outcomes may include a warning, a zero mark for the assessment, failure in the course, or a formal disciplinary hearing. Students may ask the officer about the appeal process after receiving a written decision.
