STUDENT HOUSING
Campus housing applications are submitted through the Housing Portal. Priority is given to students who live outside the local commuting area, subject to room availability and published eligibility rules. Residents must follow quiet hours from 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. Guests must be registered at reception and may not stay overnight without written approval. Maintenance issues should be reported through the Housing Portal rather than handled by residents.
