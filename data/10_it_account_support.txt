IT ACCOUNT SUPPORT
Every enrolled student receives a university email account and a Student Portal account. Students should never share their password and should enable multi-factor authentication when available. Password resets can be requested through the IT Helpdesk identity verification page. The IT Helpdesk operates Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. For urgent account lockouts during examinations, students should contact the campus IT desk in person.
